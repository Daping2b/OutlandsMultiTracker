What It Tracks

Everything Your Guild Needs to Know

One tool for the things you'd otherwise track in three different tabs.

⏱

Session Logging

Log every Dungeon run, Boating trip, or Wilderness farm with gold, doubloons, rares, and bonus XP automatically tallied per session.

📊

Log Analysis

Filter your history by character, activity, or date range to see exactly where your time has gone — and what it earned you.

⚜

Guild Management

Sync your Discord server's roles straight into OMT. Leaders and officers get a real-time view of who's online and what grade they hold.

📈

XP Tracking

See experience gains broken down by hour, day, or month for every character on your account, charted automatically.

🎁

Live Bonus Awareness

Current sanctuary and challenger dungeon bonuses are pulled in automatically, so you always know where the bonus loot is.

🔄

Auto-Updates

OMT checks for new releases on launch and updates itself in the background — no manual downloads, ever.

📤

Share with Your Guild

Upload sessions to your guild's shared pool whenever you choose, or flip on auto-upload and let every new session sync the moment you log it.

🏆

Top Sessions

See your guild's best runs ranked by gold + doubloons per hour — browse the global Top 10 or drill into rankings by location.

🤖

Discord Bot

Once linked to your server, the bot keeps guild roles and grades in sync automatically in the background — with more built directly into Discord on the way.
